Stopping distance sets the first one, so it is not the knob
    to turn when the car feels over-protective -- the second one is. Between
    the two the car is throttled in proportion to the room left, which at
    200cm means it is throttled all the time in an apartment and holding the
    trigger down does nothing. On contact: a jolt the accelerometer felt
    blocks forward travel for about a second. auto does that only
    while a behaviour is driving, on the grounds that a person felt the same
    collision themselves and does not need their controls frozen.

H.264 in an MP4, 640 wide at 15fps -- perhaps a twentieth the
    size of the stream you are watching, and it plays anywhere. It records what
    is on screen, boxes and all. Recording stops on its own at 150MB or 15
    minutes, so a forgotten one cannot fill the card.

Heading is integrated from the gyroscope, so it drifts a few
    degrees a minute: good for one manoeuvre, useless as a compass. Zero it with
    the car still. Jolt is the accelerometer noticing a collision — the bump
    switch this car does not have, and the only thing aboard that can see glass
    or a curtain. The infrared is reported but not acted on until you have
    watched both sides change and said so, because an unwired pin sits at its
    pull-up and reads exactly like a clear road.

Rotating files with a fixed ceiling, so the card cannot be
    filled even if nobody ever sweeps. The flight recorder adds a row of
    machine-readable state a few times a second, but only when something
    changed -- standing still costs almost nothing, and the interesting
    seconds come out dense. Worth turning on before a patrol run and off
    afterwards. These survive a reboot, which the system journal on this Pi
    does not.
