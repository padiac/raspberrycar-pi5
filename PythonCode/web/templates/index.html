    Sensors 
    

      

        
Jolt--

        
IR left--

        
IR right--

      

      

        
        
      

      
      
Looking down on the car. Each wedge is the last range
        reading in that direction and fades as it ages; hollow wedges are
        directions where nothing came back. Red ring: forward motion refused.
        Amber: throttled. The two dots are the corner infrared.

      
Heading is integrated from the gyroscope and drifts a few
        degrees a minute; zero it with the car still. Jolt is the accelerometer
        noticing a collision. The infrared is not acted on until you have
        watched both sides change and said so.

Stop under is a hard stop; slow under throttles in
        proportion to the room left, and is the one to lower when the car feels
        over-protective. No echo: a timed-out ping can be an empty room or glass,
        a curtain, a wall at an angle. On contact: a jolt blocks forward travel
        for about a second; auto only while a behaviour is driving.
